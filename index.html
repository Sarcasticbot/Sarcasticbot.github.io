Get started

Ready in 3 simple steps.

Get Sarcastic up and running in under two minutes.

  1. 1

    Start the bot

    Open @SarcasticXbot on Telegram and tap Start.

  2. 2

    Add it to your group

    Tap “Add me to the clan” and choose the group you want Sarcastic to serve.

  3. 3

    Grant the rights it needs

    Promote Sarcastic as admin so moderation and music features can work properly.

What permissions are needed?
Sarcastic only needs admin rights for the features you actually use:
  • Delete messages — for anti-spam and cleanup.
  • Ban / restrict users — for moderation commands like ban, mute and kick.
  • Manage voice chats — to join and play music in voice chat.
  • Invite via link — only if you use welcome/invite features.
You can skip admin entirely if you only want fun commands and downloads.
